WHO WE ARE

Your Trusted HR & Management Consulting Partner

ValuePoint Consulting Limited is a specialist Human Resources and Management Consulting firm that exists to help ambitious startups, SMEs and growing business organisations to unlock the full potential of their most critical asset i.e. their people, thereby driving measurable business value.

We partner with businesses across diverse sectors to build high-performing, people-centred organisations and also give management advice. Our approach is grounded in rigorous diagnostics, practical expertise, and a relentless commitment to delivering measurable results.

ValuePoint Consulting Limited is not just a consulting firm alone, but your strategic growth partner. We combine the agility of a boutique practice with the rigour of a world-class consulting methodology, offering our clients the best of both worlds.

Our Mission

To empower Nigerian startups, SMEs with world-class HR and Management consulting solutions so as to unlock people potential, drive operational excellence and create lasting organizational value.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted HR and management consulting partner for growing businesses across Nigeria, recognized for delivering practical, impactful and context-driven solutions.

Osayi Uuzebor Bsc, Msc, ACIPM, HRPL

Managing Consultant

Osayi is a versatile, excellence-driven HR leader with core competencies in people and culture transformation, leadership development, talent management, people analytics, learning and development as well as coaching, mentoring and counselling.

His extensive years of experience span across the Financial and Investment sectors, Real Estate, E-Commerce, FMCG, Information Technology, and Education.

Osayi is a certified Human Resource professional and an Associate Member of the Chartered Institute of Personnel Management of Nigeria (ACIPM).

He holds a Bachelor's degree in Business Administration from the University of Benin and a Master's degree in Organizational Behaviour from the University of Lagos.

Passionate about learning and sharing knowledge, he has facilitated trainings, attended several professional courses, and founded the Society for Organizational Behavior.

He is dedicated to developing leaders at all levels and empowering young people to navigate socio-economic challenges.

As part of his commitment to society, Osayi volunteers with the Lagos Food Bank Initiative (LFBI), supporting disadvantaged communities through food and welfare outreach.

HR Advisory Lead

With extensive experience across industries like telecom, towerco, business consulting, fintech, oil & gas and healthcare. Key Expertise

  • Financial modeling: Builds annual operational budgets, monthly reports, project finance models, asset depreciation, and inventory systems with variance analysis and visualizations.

  • Training: Facilitates sessions on financial modeling, marketing analytics, Excel spreadsheets, and entrepreneurial strategy.
  • Business strategy: Provides start-up advisory, operations strategy, and entrepreneurial trainings.

Tinuke Bosun-Okusaga MCIPM, HRPL, SPHRi

Talent Acquisition Manager

Tinuke is a Management Consultant with over Eighteen (18yrs) experience. Her experience spans across Financial services, Management Education and Consulting. She is currently the Managing Consultant for PeoplePlus Consulting; an indigenous Human Resources and Strategy Consulting firm she founded.

. Prior to this, she consulted for IHS towers; one of the largest independent owners, operators and developers of shared telecommunications infrastructure in the world. Her consulting journey started at PricewaterhouseCoopers (PwC) Nigeria where she was a Manager in the People and Organisation Advisory practice for about Four years. Before joining PwC, she had hands on HR experience at the Lagos Business School where she served for almost nine (9) years.

She sits on the Board of St. Saviour’s School, Ebute-Meta, DoTheDream Youth Development Initiative and ENIPRO Training Academy. She serves as a Mentor for Women in Management, Business and Public Service (WIMBIZ), Full-time MBA students of Lagos Business School and Teach for Nigeria (TFN) Foundation. She is also an Assessor for Enterprise Nigeria.

Tinuke is a Certified Management Trainer by Centre for Management Development (CMD), Certified HR Practitioner by the Chartered Institute for Personnel Management (CIPM), and a Senior Professional in Human Resources (SPHRi) certification from the Human Resources Certification Institute (HRCI). She is a member of the Society for Human Resources Management(SHRM) and Association for MBAs (AMBA).

She holds a Bachelors degree in Agricultural Economics and Extension from Federal University of Technology Akure and an Executive MBA from the prestigious Lagos Business School.

She is passionate about courses that promote education especially for the underprivileged and helping young people discover themselves and chart their professional course.
