Who We Are

ValuePoint Consulting Limited is a specialist Human Resource and Management Consulting firm that exist to help ambitious SMEs organisations unlock the full potential of their most critical asset, their people, thereby driving measurable business value.


We partner with businesses across diverse sectors to build high-performing, people-centred organisations and give management advice. Our approach is grounded in rigorous diagnostics, practical expertise, and a relentless commitment to delivering results that can be measured.

ValuePoint is not just a consulting firm alone, but your strategic growth partner. We combine the agility of a boutique practice with the rigour of a world-class consulting methodology, offering our clients the best of both worlds.
